          |-- [FACT] (Msg ID: 21) NumPy's data type promotion rules: when adding arrays of different integer types (e.g., `int8` and `uint8`, or `int8` and `int16`), the result is promoted to `int16` to accommodate the full range of possible values and prevent overflow.
